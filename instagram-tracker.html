How to use

Three steps to get your data

1
Request your Instagram data
  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile
  2. Tap the menu (☰) → Settings → Accounts Center
  3. Tap "Your information and permissions" → "Download your information"
  4. Select your Instagram account
  5. Tap "Customize information" and select only Followers and following
  6. Tap "Date range" and set it to All time — selecting a shorter range gives incomplete results
  7. Tap "Format" and set it to JSON, not HTML
  8. Submit — Instagram will email you when the file is ready, usually within a few minutes up to 48 hours
2
Get your files
  1. Open the email from Instagram and download the ZIP file
  2. Extract the ZIP — inside you'll find a folder with several files
  3. You only need these four files:
📄 followers_1.json 📄 following.json 📄 recently_unfollowed_profiles.json 📄 recent_follow_requests.json
  1. Ignore everything else in the folder
3
Drop them into the app
  1. Click "Open Tracker" below
  2. Drag and drop all 4 files at once into the upload area
  3. Your results will appear instantly
💡 Tips
  • Always use "All time" as the date range or your followers list will be incomplete
  • Re-download your data regularly to keep results fresh
  • Your files never leave your device — everything runs in your browser
